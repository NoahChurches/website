Lessons

Noah has extensive experience, having taught for over 10 years in a range of settings; privately, at schools, in homes, in small groups, one-on-one, and across all ages (from as young as 5yrs old to retirees). As a multi instrumentalist, his approach is more holistic, incorporating singing and movement to instill the fundamental part of music. The feeling and the idea behind the music. Go beyond the simple rote learning of "put that finger there, the pick that string like this" and instead learn the reasons behind how to play and what makes music work. Noah is a big fan of improvising and has expereince in a broad range of styles, so there is always something new to learn. While he primarily teaches guitar, he is also a mandolin and piano teacher. Please feel free to get in touch.
